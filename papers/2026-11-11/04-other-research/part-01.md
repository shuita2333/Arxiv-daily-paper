### 1. [Short-Form Video Exposure and Academic Strain: A Dose-Response Analysis and Acquiescence Diagnosis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09899)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Md. Alamgir Hossain  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Short-form video platforms occupy a substantial share of undergraduate leisure time, raising concerns about sustained attention, deep learning, and academic well-being. However, much existing evidence relies on single-source Likert instruments with limited attention to response-style bias and measurement validity. This study analyses 863 university students who completed a 67-item, seven-construct battery assessing short-form content addiction, attention fragmentation, notification overload, cognitive fatigue, deep learning engagement, academic burnout, and self-regulation, alongside categorical exposure measures. A layered analytical protocol was implemented in Python, including descriptive screening, dimensionality assessment, reliability, convergent validity, careless-response diagnostics, and known-groups criterion validity. Daily exposure and in-study checking frequency showed consistent monotonic associations with maladaptive outcomes. Short-form content addiction exhibited the strongest effect ($\eta^2=0.117$, $\rho=0.273$, $p<0.001$), supported by nonparametric tests. However, the measurement analyses revealed substantial validity concerns: parallel analysis retained a single factor per construct, average variance extracted did not exceed 0.31, and a general agreement factor explained 59--89\% of construct variance. Controlling for this factor reversed the association between addiction and deep-learning engagement from $+0.55$ to $-0.49$. These findings indicate that short-form video exposure is associated with cognitive and academic strain, while response-style contamination can substantially distort theoretically meaningful relationships. The results highlight the need for balanced item wording, embedded response-quality controls, and explicit response-style modelling in behavioural-addiction research.

### 2. [CaregiverConnect: A Mobile Application for Stress Tracking and Support in Dementia Care](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09901)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Yagna Manasa Boyapati, Arshia Khan  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> The rising global prevalence of dementia places escalating demands on paid professional caregivers, who frequently experience severe stress and professional isolation. Existing mobile health (mHealth) applications rarely integrate real-time stress monitoring with multimodal relief strategies. CaregiverConnect, a Flutter/Firebase mobile application comprising nine feature modules, was evaluated in a single-group, pre-post pilot study with 10 paid professional dementia caregivers at two nursing home facilities over two weeks. Descriptive analysis yielded an exploratory effect estimate for self-reported stress change (Cohen's d = 1.60; interpreted with caution given N = 10 and no control group), an improvement in emotional support responses from 60% to 100% of participants, and near-perfect usability ratings (UI Navigation: 4.90/5.0). These preliminary findings support the feasibility and acceptability of CaregiverConnect and identify end-of-shift stress peaks as a priority target for future adaptive design. Larger randomized controlled trials are needed to establish efficacy.

### 3. [Human-AI Collaboration at Scale: Task Criticality, Agency, and Friction across 250,000 Conversations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09902)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Yijia Shao, Dora Zhao, Vishakh Padmakumar 等 5 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> AI is increasingly embedded in professional work, yet how people actually collaborate with it at scale remains poorly understood. Drawing on a corpus of 249,834 real-world conversations, we conduct privacy-preserving analysis by asking three questions: what work people bring to AI, what roles humans retain in completing that work, and where human-AI collaboration breaks down. We find that users bring to AI not only trivial tasks but also consequential, hard-to-reverse work, with high criticality delegation concentrating in advisory and professional domains. As stakes rise, users engage more actively with AI output, and conversation length nearly doubles from ephemeral to high-stake tasks. We then examine human agency, the degree of control and responsibility users retain in completing the task. Human-led collaboration dominates overall, but users construct their level of agency through how they prompt and iterate. Across different levels of agency lies a common tension: does working with AI build human capability, or substitute for human effort? We examine this tension through AI-supported learning and upskilling, finding that active teaching is common (67% of conversations) but its benefits are unevenly realized. We further show that friction arises in roughly half of all conversations but it is often productive, and users often employ active strategies to recover from friction when it does occur. Together, these results map how humans and AI work together at scale and when that collaboration makes people more capable, and when it doesn't.

### 4. [Interpretive Proactivity: Expanding the Design Space of Proactive Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09904)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Pengqi Wang, Emily Kuang  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Research on proactive systems in HCI has grown substantially, but the discussion has centered on systems that proactively generate actions, recommendations, and anticipatory responses for users. We identify a second paradigm of proactive behavior that is just as widespread and far less examined. Input method editors, auto-correction, and similar text-entry systems take initiative on every keystroke, not to generate new content but to infer, interpret, and commit to meanings from partial user input. We call the first paradigm generative proactivity and the second interpretive proactivity, and we argue that this second paradigm deserves a place of its own in the proactivity research agenda. Using multilingual input and auto-correction as case studies, we show that interpretive proactivity inherits the vocabulary of proactive design but changes what that vocabulary refers to. Timing becomes a question of when to commit an interpretation rather than when to interrupt, and control calls for the ability to reinterpret committed input rather than only accept or reject a suggestion. We close by arguing that the cost of errors should drive the design of interpretive systems, and posing open questions for the workshop.

### 5. [Facilitating Reproducibility in Visualization Systems Research Through Containerization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09910)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Thomas Breimer, Pirm Dhararag, Kiya Gedamu 等 12 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> In this position paper, we argue for the importance of containerizing visual analytics artifacts to improve the scientific progress of our research community. First, based on our previous experience authoring, reading, and reviewing visual analytics research from the previous decade, we identify risks and impediments to empirical research in visual analytics within the broader reproducibility crisis, including an inability to compare systems and techniques and a slow pace of adjusting to a rapidly changing ecosystem of analysis methods, such as machine learning and artificial intelligence. Next, we analyze the benefits of different artifact sharing and deployment setups across five artifact goals: reproducibility, replicability, portability, performance, and scalability. Then, we argue that containerization addresses the issue of reproducibility in visual analytics research, with downstream benefits in terms of replicability, portability, performance, and scalability. To demonstrate the ease of use and the utility of containerization, we deploy ten visual analytics systems developed over the course of a graduate seminar onto a live server using containerization with the help of agentic AI. We share lessons learned through the containerization process, and describe a suite of potential benefits for our research community as well as research opportunities for leveraging modularity to improve the artifacts themselves.

### 6. [Reaching the last mile? How Design Decisions Shape Medical Device Qualification and Classification of Patient Support Chatbots in the EU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09911)

**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Few AI chatbots have transitioned the 'last mile' from research prototypes to clinical practice, partly because mapping conversational design decisions to regulatory requirements is complicated. This paper presents a design-oriented regulatory analysis of a chatbot supporting stroke survivors. Using Design Space Analysis operationalised through the QOC (Questions, Options, Criteria) framework, HCI researchers and legal scholars assess how variations in three conversational functions - health Q\&A, recovery monitoring, and motivating medical adherence - influence qualification and classification under the European Medical Device Regulation (MDR), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and AI Act (AIA). The paper contributes insights into ten conversational design considerations influencing regulatory interpretation and demonstrates how QOC can support integrating legal considerations into human-centred design workflows. Finally, we suggest compliance-aware pathways to market, while reflecting on whether all chatbots should pursue the 'last mile'.

### 9. [Benefits and Burdens of Technology-Supported Activity Pacing in ME/CFS: A Thematic Analysis of Reddit Discussions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09915)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Martin Matych, Michal Dolezel  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Technology and self-tracking play an increasingly important role in the self-management of chronic conditions. Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME/CFS) is a type of energy-limiting condition, with activity pacing as a key self-management strategy that helps individuals regulate their activity within their energy limits to prevent symptom worsening. However, people with ME/CFS frequently adopt technologies designed for healthy bodies, which they have to repurpose to suit their pacing needs. We report findings from a thematic analysis of Reddit data, using the Non-Adoption, Abandonment, Scale-Up, Spread and Sustainability (NASSS) framework as a sensitising concept. Among these, affordability was a salient topic, highlighting the importance of making technology for pacing financially accessible to people with ME/CFS who follow the high-tech route.

### 11. [Made in the Image of the Creator? AI Use in Creative Work Reduces Authenticity, Love & Other Psychological Benefits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09918)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Jessica Carson, Paul H. P. Hanel, Wijnand A. P. Van Tilburg  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Across psychological, philosophical, and theological traditions, creations have been understood as being "made in the image of their creator" - they reflect the person who made them. The rise of generative artificial intelligence, however, has blurred the throughline between creator and creation. Across three experiments (one pre-registered, U.S. and U.K. samples; N = 651), we examined differences in creator outcomes associated with AI-assisted versus human-only creative work. Compared with human-only work, AI-assisted work was associated with significantly lower self- and identity-relevant indicators, including authenticity, self-creation connection, positive affect, and intrinsic motivation, and with higher levels of the "existential ick" emotions of disgust, guilt, and shame. Additionally, we found that the authenticity gap was statistically mediated by the creators' perception that their AI-assisted work contains less of their love, above and beyond differences in perceived effort, uniqueness, quality, and expensiveness. We also ruled out effort justification as an alternative explanation. Together, these findings suggest that AI reshapes not only how creative work is produced, but also the extent to which creators experience their work as an authentic expression and extension of themselves.

### 12. [Cellery: Gradual, Composable Structure for Spreadsheets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09920)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Jacob Yim, Sathwika Peechara, Brian Hempel 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Structure in spreadsheets is typically restricted to ranges of cells and flat tables. However, replacing the open-ended two-dimensional grid with a more structured alternative risks limiting users' freedom and impeding exploratory workflows. How can we enable expressive structure in spreadsheets while preserving their flexibility? We propose a model that allows users to gradually add structure on top of the traditional grid. To identify common structural patterns, we conducted a content analysis of 30 real-world spreadsheets. Based on our findings, we developed Cellery, a novel spreadsheet system that makes these structures explicit. In Cellery, users gradually define the structure of their spreadsheets using named groupings of cells, composing together four structural primitives: Atoms, Lists, Tables, and Blocks. Once defined, users can directly manipulate structures, dynamically reference them in formulas, and incorporate them in reusable abstractions. We evaluate Cellery with an expressiveness study reproducing spreadsheets from our sample, demonstrating that it is capable of representing types of structure that traditional spreadsheets cannot. In a preliminary user study of five participants, we find that they successfully use Cellery to design spreadsheets with explicit structure, and we uncover opportunities for further work on the formula language.

### 13. [Grounding Insight: An Artificial Affect Signal for Human-Centric Explainability of RL Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09922)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Bernhard Hilpert, Kim Baraka, Joost Broekens  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> In Human-Interactive Robot Learning (HIRL), human teachers assess learning progress by observing agent behavior. However, Reinforcement Learning (RL) agents often fail to express learning progress in human-interpretable ways, leading to suboptimal or counterproductive teaching. This work proposes "Insight": a human-interpretable affective signal grounded in the RL process. It is specifically designed to address the "Absence of learning" human teacher mental model misalignment - the misconception that a failing or exploring agent is broken, underpowered or not learning at all - by signaling an agent's "Aha!" Insight moment. The signal is derived from the absolute Temporal Difference (TD) error making it easily implementable in other RL agents. To ground this signal, five design criteria were formulated: Relevance, Interpretability, Faithfulness, Scalability, Generalizability. The structural construct validity was assessed through simulation, examining micro and macro dynamics and event-related sensitivity. Micro dynamics reveal that Insight captures meaningful learning events at the appropriate time and magnitude. Macro dynamics demonstrate that the signal follows the agent's learning phases and reflects overall progress. Event-related sensitivity shows, the signal distinguishes the saliency of environment interactions, such as positive rewards versus "trauma-like" negative events. These results demonstrate that technical RL dynamics can be effectively packaged into human-like affective substrates to close the teacher-learner loop.

### 14. [Fairness Explanations Improve Fairness In Human-AI Decision Making: an empirical evaluation of parole decision-making](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09924)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Peter Daish, Alan Dix, Muneeb Ahmad 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Both humans and AI exhibit systemic biases, yet little is known about how interface design can foster fairness in hybrid human-AI decision-making. This paper introduces a method for extracting fair data samples from biased datasets and adapts a fairness-explanation technique to highlight potential AI bias during decisions. We conducted a within-subjects study (n=48) where non-experts made decisions across three conditions: independent human judgment, unexplained AI assistance, and AI assistance augmented with visual ethnic-bias explanations (Fair-XAI). ### 15. [MAGE: Towards Generalizable Multi-timescale EEG Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09928)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Tuan-Kiet Doan, Van-Chien Nguyen, Trung-Hieu Tran 等 10 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Electroencephalography (EEG) data plays an important role in understanding human brain activity, with various major applications. While current approaches, which largely build on EEG foundation models, have demonstrated strong performance across datasets and tasks, they typically rely on a fixed temporal scale. This design choice limits their ability to capture characteristics of neural dynamic behavior, often spanning across multiple timescales, which are naturally captured in EEG recordings. To address this limitation, we propose MAGE (\textbf{Multi-timescale Adaptive Generalization for EEG}), a foundation model that dynamically adapts to capture and integrate information across different timescales. Specifically, MAGE combines two key mechanisms: (i) a novel multi-timescale attention mechanism, capable of learning representations across multiple temporal scales and (ii) a trainable aggregation module, that dynamically identifies and adapts to the most informative timescale of the input signal. Experimental results across seven datasets demonstrate that MAGE consistently outperforms existing EEG foundation models while using substantially fewer parameters. Moreover, our results support the hypothesis that different downstream tasks benefit from distinct temporal scales. All source code will be released.

### 16. [Fresh Enough to Decide: Age of Intelligence for Cost-Aware Model Synchronization in Dynamic IoT Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09935)

**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Resource-constrained IoT devices cache learned models for real-time decisions, but frequent updates incur substantial bandwidth, energy, and service overhead. Because decision-relevant model differences need not grow with elapsed time, we define Age of Intelligence (AoInt) as a model-version-relative, client-specific, and action utility distortion, and formulate synchronization as a tradeoff between stale-decision regret and refresh cost. ### 17. [Topic-Aware Multi-Task Mental Health Classification with Active Learning-Based Stepwise Relabeling for Social Media Conversations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09941)

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**

Social media platforms like Reddit have become spaces where people openly share their experiences, but the support is often provided by individuals without clinical training. The results suggest that the proposed framework significantly reduces label noise in multi-task mental health classification, and that topic information provides additional context.

### 19. [Confusion-Adaptive Margin Learning for Handwritten Letter Recognition: A Controlled Study on EMNIST Letters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09953)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Azam Nouri  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Handwritten character recognition systems can achieve high accuracy while retaining concentrated errors among visually similar classes. Motivated by residual letter confusions in earlier geometry-based recognition work, we investigate whether these errors can be addressed through the training objective. We introduce Confusion-Adaptive Margin Learning (CAML), which constructs a sparse weighted confusion graph from a discovery partition held out from pilot training and applies pair-specific cosine-separation constraints during final training. CAML adds no classifier, routing rule, or inference-time parameters.
We evaluate CAML on EMNIST Letters against a strictly matched cross-entropy CNN baseline, using the same architecture, final training data, deterministic augmentation, optimizer, and checkpoint selection. Eight confusion pairs are targeted: I/L, G/Q, U/V, R/V, D/O, C/E, I/J, and H/N. On the official clean test set, accuracy changes from 95.6346% to 95.6731%, and macro-F1 from 0.956326 to 0.956724. Mean accuracy across ten predefined corrupted conditions increases from approximately 95.3812% to 95.4303%; symmetric errors within targeted pairs decrease from 614 to 605 (1.47%).
The global differences are small and not statistically reliable. CAML fixes 124 baseline errors but introduces 116 new errors on clean data (exact McNemar p=0.6515), and no corruption-specific difference remains significant after Holm correction. Thus, CAML does not establish global superiority, but shows that a leakage-controlled, pair-specific margin objective can alter targeted error structure with limited overall benefit for an already strong classifier.

### 20. [DiffusedSplitFed: Latent Diffusion-based Obfuscation for Reconstruction-Resilient Split Federated Medical Image Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09954)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Chamani Shiranthika, Parvaneh Saeedi  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Federated Learning (FL), Split Learning (SL), and Split Federated Learning (SplitFed) enable collaborative medical image analysis without sharing raw patient data. To address this vulnerability, we propose \textbf{DiffusedSplitFed}, a SplitFed framework that integrates Latent Denoising Diffusion Models (LDDMs) at client--server split points to obfuscate transmitted representations while preserving segmentation performance. Experiments on three medical imaging datasets (HAM10K, Blastocysts, and FHPsAOPMSB), supported by multiple independent runs and paired statistical analyses, demonstrate improved resistance to the evaluated image reconstruction attacks compared with Baseline SplitFed, while maintaining competitive segmentation performance. Comparisons with existing diffusion-based segmentation methods further demonstrate competitive segmentation accuracy under the evaluated settings. A conditional convergence analysis is also provided to characterize the effects of LDDM-induced gradient perturbations and local optimization on training. These findings highlight the potential of latent diffusion-based representation obfuscation for reconstruction-resilient collaborative medical image segmentation.
