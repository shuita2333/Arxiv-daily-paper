### 551. [The Privacy Cost of Writing an Audit Receipt Before Its Context Is Known](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31122)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Faruk Alpay, Taylan Alpay  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Cryptography and Security

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> An audit recorder must permit exact recovery with a small private index before it knows which questions a future reader will ask. A recursion on question partitions generates the decision trees, their eventwise costs and the price equations. A common-optimal-tree criterion explains when reduction to a fractional cover preserves the result. On uniform binary linear event spaces with four private roles, if every quartet is split evenly by some bit, optimal identification probability at expected follow-up budget $b$ is $(1+b)/2$. Otherwise it is $\min(1/2+b/3,3/4)$. We characterize all priors attaining the latter curve in its rising regime and derive a closed-form compiler for three binary fields. For a population tilted by one pairwise parity we obtain the full budget curve and a single recorder attaining it. On eight negotiated TLS suites the aligned populations allow a 60 percent follow-up rate at a 70 percent identification ceiling. A pairwise correlation with unchanged marginal field frequencies reduces that maximum to 40 percent. Across 960 local TLS sessions, the recorder recovered every negotiated suite using two private bits per session.

### 552. [Site-Conditioned Boundary Closure for Subsurface Seepage Forecasting and Relief Placement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31125)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Edward Holmberg, Elias Ioup, Mahdi Abdelguerfi  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Truncating a large heterogeneous diffusion model around a localized, threshold-activated outlet saves degrees of freedom but severs exterior feedback that governs when the outlet opens and how much it discharges. By separating forcing-driven background head from local withdrawal drawdown, Site-Conditioned Boundary Closure (SCBC) maps local physical descriptors to positive exchange coefficients at the cut boundary. Supervised on unforced donor-domain withdrawal responses, these coefficients yield a passivity-preserving reduced operator that generalizes across unseen forcing histories without refitting. We evaluate SCBC on flood-driven subsurface seepage beneath levee foundations against full-grid reference simulations (MODFLOW~6). Across three held-out foundation panels, a single frozen SCBC tree estimator reduced peak-discharge log RMSE by 26.1%, 30.9% and 42.6% relative to identical local patches using a shared boundary coefficient. We then test whether better screening forecasts improve constrained placement, minimizing peak landside toe head subject to a discharge capacity limit and a budget of three sequential full-grid checks. On a separate 36-foundation test using a coarser 30-state ring operator, a neural SCBC estimator improved discharge forecasts across candidates yet selected four over-capacity placements before checks, compared with one for a shared-coefficient ring, which recovered all 144 reference optima after three checks.

### 553. [CrownFusion: 3D Dental Crown Generation Using Geometry Images and Latent Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31128)

**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> We introduce CrownFusion, a latent diffusion model to generate 3D dental crown designs from geometry images, a 2D-grid encoding of surface coordinates. Departing from traditional approaches that use point clouds, our "CrownImage" representation not only unlocks the full power of image-based generative architectures, but also preserves fine geometric detail by operating at far higher spatial resolutions than point clouds to alleviate smoothing artifacts in the 3D generation. Conditioned on jointly learned embeddings of the surrounding dentition, our probabilistic diffusion model produces anatomically plausible crowns with morphological variations tailored to individual patient cases. To accommodate multiple valid designs, we propose an occlusal fit metric based on proximity to the antagonist teeth. We evaluate our method through quantitative experiments and qualitative studies involving expert testimonials. We also release the FDI 16 Crown Dataset, comprising 11,513 annotated intra-oral scans, to support future research in dental CAD.

### 554. [Serialization and the Character-System: Measuring Narrative Concentration in French Fiction (1800--1940)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31133)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Jean Barré, Antoine Bourgois, Thierry Poibeau  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computation and Language

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Every novel divides a finite quantity of narrative attention among its characters, and whether the shape of that division, between a narrative organized around a single figure and one spread across a crowd, has a history is far less settled than the abundance of critical commentary on protagonists might suggest. We operationalize Woloch's notion of the character-system, the distribution of narrative attention across a novel's cast structured by the tension between "the one" and "the many", as the inequality (Gini coefficient) of the PageRank distribution over a character network. We trace this measure of structural concentration across 12,011 French novels published between 1800 and 1940, drawn from the Hors d'usage corpus of Gallica fiction. Over the long nineteenth century the trajectory is U-shaped, with concentration declining to a trough in the 1850s-1860s and rising again through the turn of the century. The change is small, comparable to the difference between a novel built on two co-equal protagonists and one built on a single protagonist, but it is robust to the character cutoff, corpus density, cast size, and author effects. Detective and adventure fiction are less concentrated than the average novel of their period. Literary history reads the roman-feuilleton either as the novel of a superior hero or as a form of proliferating episodes, and on this structural measure our results support the second reading. Across the age of the feuilleton (1840-1914), authors whose careers were structured by newspaper serialization distribute centrality more evenly than a literary control, net of period, genre, and canon. Because such claims are only as trustworthy as the networks they rest on, we also evaluate the extraction pipeline against manually annotated novels, at the levels of cast and character identity.

### 555. [SRCWDNet: Semantic-Relational Cross-Window Dual-Graph Network for Multi-Label Remote Sensing Scene Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31135)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Fareeha Amir  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Multi-label remote sensing scene classification remains a challenging task due to complex spatial-spectral patterns, extreme class imbalance, and inter-label dependencies that are often insufficiently modeled. To address these challenges, we introduce a Semantic-Relational Cross-Window Dual-Graph Network (SRCWDNet), which integrates three key modules: (1) a Cross-Window Attention module that captures local and global visual context through learnable global tokens; (2) a Dual Label-Graph module that adaptively combines statistical co-occurrence graphs and learned semantic-embedding graphs to facilitate label-relational reasoning; and (3) a Neural Classifier Chain that sequentially predicts labels to exploit inter-label dependencies. Evaluated using five-fold cross-validation and trained with an Asymmetric-Graph-Consistency loss, SRCWDNet achieves accuracies of 98.93% and 96.53%, with corresponding F1-scores of 0.9365 and 0.8723 on the MLRSNet and China-MAS50k datasets, respectively, outperforming CNN, transformer, and lightweight baselines. These findings demonstrate that jointly modeling visual attention and adaptive label correlations can substantially improve multi-label remote sensing scene classification, particularly under long-tailed class distributions.

### 556. [HoloSplat: Real-Time Token Streaming for Feed-Forward Human Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31140)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Chaofan Li, Qihan Ding, Shihan Wang 等 9 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Live free-viewpoint video streaming of humans enables immersive telepresence and interactive XR applications. However, this requires reconstructing each frame in real time, transmitting the data within strict bandwidth budgets, and decoding it on lightweight client GPUs. Existing approaches, whether relying on per-frame optimization or feed-forward inference, generate massive 3D Gaussian payloads that exceed practical streaming limits, while aggressive compression severely degrades rendering quality. In this work, we present HoloSplat, a real-time streaming framework for human-centric volumetric video. HoloSplat splits a feed-forward reconstructor before its Gaussian head, introducing a multi-rate streaming codec driven by server-side token compression and client-side token restoration. On the server side, we apply opacity-guided token pruning, PCA-based dimensionality reduction, and lossless geometry coding for token anchors. On the client side, a feature reconstruction hypernetwork is fused into the Gaussian head to recover compression-induced quality loss. Furthermore, we introduce Neural Session Calibration (NSC), which rapidly fine-tunes the Gaussian head online using incoming camera views during a warm-up phase. This resolves quality degradation from novel camera parameters and varying capture conditions with zero per-frame overhead. In our evaluations, HoloSplat averages 32.2 FPS on four RTX 5090 GPUs at 50 Mbit/s. It decodes each frame into 3D Gaussians in 22.1 ms on an RTX 3050 Ti laptop GPU, transmitting 22.8$\times$ less data than uncompressed float32 Gaussians, while calibrated decoding improves PSNR by 1.050 dB.

### 557. [Head Anything: Correspondence-Guided Joint Generation of 3D Heads from Few Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31143)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Umut Kocasari, Bartłomiej Baranowski, Richard Shaw 等 5 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Large image-to-3D generators recover detailed heads from a single photograph but treat every image as a new object. For several images of one person, they hallucinate unseen regions independently without using information from the other images. We present Head Anything, which generates detailed head meshes from a few images with changing expressions and viewpoints. Instead of training a multi-image prior, we turn a pretrained single-image generator with a native 3D latent space into a joint generator that denoises all images together. Since global attention between images is hard to learn from scarce 3D head data, lightweight correspondence-attention adapters let every 3D token attend only to its corresponding points in the other images. We obtain correspondences by fitting a neural parametric head model to the generator's own single-image outputs, requiring no ground truth at test time. Training on both ground-truth and fitted correspondences makes the adapters robust to fitting errors. Each mesh stays pixel-aligned with its own image and borrows the unseen regions from all others. On head scans, Head Anything lowers the head Chamfer distance of the best image-to-3D generator by 32% and its cross-frame inconsistency by 47%, and its meshes improve face tracking. Code and models will be released.

### 558. [Tokens as Locality-Preserving Anchors: Scalable Novel View Synthesis with Target-Adaptive Token Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31156)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Sagnik Ghosh, Aswin C. Sankaranarayanan  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Transformer-based view synthesis models render each target viewpoint from the source images, without an explicit 3D representation, which lets them reproduce fine detail and view-dependent effects. However, their decoder attends to every source token for every frame, so rendering slows with each source image. Rasterization avoids this by culling primitives that the viewpoint does not see, but source tokens cannot be culled this way, as they have no explicit position or extent. Our key insight is that source tokens are nonetheless spatial anchors: each encodes a local region of the scene, so the decoder's attention for any viewpoint concentrates on a few of them. A lightweight router predicts these tokens from the source tokens and the viewpoint, and the decoder attends only to a fixed budget of top-ranked tokens, so its cost no longer depends on the number of source tokens. We supervise the router with the decoder's attention, using non-maximum suppression to demote redundant tokens. With 24 source images and two views' worth of tokens, our model renders 6.4 times faster than LagerNVS, at a PSNR only 0.68 dB lower. One ranking serves every budget, so the budget can change at render time.

### 559. [SPROUT: Predicting Objective-Grounded Prompt Utility for Efficient Text-to-Image Preference Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31163)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Kangping Hu, Akshay Mehra, Stephen Mussmann  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Preference tuning of text-to-image diffusion models spends most of its compute generating candidate images, yet training prompts are typically sampled uniformly at random, wasting compute generating images that provide too little signal for model alignment. To best utilize the available compute, we study prompt selection, where at each iteration we identify the most useful prompts for preference tuning. We separate prompt selection into a utility score, an estimator of that utility score, and batch selection. Within this framework, we propose Selection by PRedicting Objective-grounded UTility (SPROUT). The utility score is predicted on every prompt in the pool, including unseen prompts, by a text-only model, refit after every iteration on all utilities observed so far. A randomized acceptance rule then turns these predictions into a batch, so no images are generated beyond those used for training. For direct group preference optimization (DGPO), the objective-grounded utility is the norm of the gradient that the objective produces from a prompt's group of images. Fine-tuning Stable Diffusion 1.5 with DGPO on GenEval, SPROUT matches the final score of uniform sampling in about 44% of the iterations and improves the area under the learning curve with respect to both uniform sampling and CGPO, a recent variance-based prompt sampler. The efficiency gains hold under four held-out reward models never used in training, and the ranking of methods carries over to DPG-Bench and T2I-CompBench++. Our code is available at this https URL.

### 560. [Analyzing and Improving FlowMap Distillation of Autoregressive Video Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31164)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Yong-Hyun Park, Guande He, Joon Hyeok Kim 等 8 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> FlowMap distillation offers a natural route to few-step generative modeling by directly learning a teacher's denoising trajectories. Yet for autoregressive (AR) video generation, existing few-step models still rely on distribution matching distillation (DMD) to remain competitive, sacrificing sample diversity and requiring a large bidirectional teacher. In this work, we analyze what limits standalone AR FlowMap distillation and introduce two complementary guidance mechanisms to close this gap: (1) anti-drifting guidance, which helps the AR teacher correct drifted student rollouts by training an auxiliary model to mimic student drift and using it as a negative guidance branch; and (2) motion-aware guidance, which makes motion easier for a FlowMap student to learn by locating motion patches and selectively applying skip-layer guidance. Empirically, our approach applies to $\pi$-Flow, MeanFlow, and consistency distillation with 1.3B AR teachers, improving generation quality and reducing drift over 30 s rollouts. Notably, our 4-step $\pi$-Flow students match DMD students distilled from 14B teachers on VBench while preserving the sample diversity of their AR teachers, demonstrating that pure FlowMap distillation is effective for few-step autoregressive video generation.

### 561. [Reducing Order Sensitivity in Decision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31174)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Bodong Liu, Dongyoung Go, Sofian Zalouk 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Artificial Intelligence

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Decision models are emerging as fast components of larger AI systems. Yet recent decision models, such as Jev, process options sequentially, leading to different predictions based on their ordering. Across ten open- and closed-source decision models, we find that \emph{order-sensitivity} is pervasive, causing substantial accuracy degradation and unreliable decision-making. These effects become more pronounced as the number of options grows and can compound across multi-step decisions. To address this problem, we systematically compare eight methods for mitigating order sensitivity in terms of robustness, predictive accuracy, and inference efficiency. Among these methods, we find that \emph{tree attention} is a surprisingly effective and efficient method. It achieves order invariance by processing options as parallel branches of a shared context. When used during training, it matches the baseline's average accuracy. Applied to the baseline without retraining, it also eliminates order sensitivity with only a 1.1-point drop in accuracy. Our findings demonstrate that order invariance can be enforced directly in decision models with little or no loss in predictive accuracy.

### 562. [Code-Based Message Authentication with Statistical Guarantees via Coordinate Selection and Masking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31178)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Hsuan-Po Liu, Hessam Mahdavifar  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Cryptography and Security

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> We study message authentication over a public channel against a computationally unbounded adversary. Motivated by low-complexity applications, we consider schemes that leverage off-the-shelf binary linear codes already used for error correction in communication systems. Existing code-based constructions derive an authentication tag primarily from a single symbol in a $q$-ary code, which limits the tag length. However, selecting several binary coordinates allows a fixed binary linear code to support different tag lengths. We characterize how coordinate selection and ordering affect the statistical guarantees against impersonation and substitution attacks. In particular, we explore three methods: the subset selector, the ordered selector, and the independent selector. They read uniformly chosen coordinates in a fixed public or a secret random order, or with replacement, and add a uniform mask. With a fresh key per message, all three achieve the optimal impersonation probability $2^{-l_t}$ for $l_t$-bit tags. We bound the subset selector's substitution probability using only nonzero weights. Those of the other two depend only on the code length $n$, $l_t$, and the nonzero weight farthest from $n/2$. The independent selector achieves $2^{-l_t}$ for substitution when all nonzero weights equal $n/2$. Finally, we evaluate first-order Reed-Muller and Bose-Chaudhuri-Hocquenghem dual subcodes.

### 563. [Learning Neurovascular Codes for EEG-to-fMRI Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31194)

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> In this paper, we introduce Neurovascular Codes as shared EEG representations for fMRI generation. We first train a separate EEG encoder to predict each fMRI signal, learning target-specific temporal and spectral features from the same EEG window. After training, we freeze the encoders and project their concatenated hidden features into a shared neurovascular code. Conditioned on this code, stochastic interpolant transforms Gaussian noise into a vector of fMRI signals, allowing different responses to be sampled for the same EEG window. The corresponding improvements over the strongest generator baselines are 18.8% and 47.9%. Beyond reconstruction accuracy, we also evaluate the generated signals using subject-identification classifiers trained on real fMRI. Our method achieves the highest mean accuracy among the evaluated generators. We hope that our work opens up new opportunities for cross-modal brain modeling.

### 564. [Matching Under Metric Privacy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31208)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Hongyi B. Hu, Dung Nguyen, M. Amin Rahimian  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> We study how to represent and randomize points in $d$-dimensional Euclidean space to obtain accurate bipartite matchings under metric privacy. Given $n$ private and $n$ public points, we analyze local mechanisms that randomize each private point or its distances to the public points. Using the public points' affine span, we construct a smaller representation that preserves all matching costs. When a trusted server receives the private points, we establish privacy and utility guarantees for Gumbel matching, which randomizes edge costs and releases only the assignment. Through upper bounds and worst-case constructions, we establish the tight rate $\Theta\!\left(n\min\{d,\log n\}/\varepsilon\right)$ for expected cost above the nonprivate optimum. Benchmark experiments support $d\approx\log n$ as a practical guide to the transition between directly randomizing point coordinates and using Gumbel matching. Extensions to squared Euclidean costs enable private nonparametric two-sample hypothesis testing. These results show how the application's trust model guides representation and dimension choices for better utility: under local privacy, low-dimensional point representations reduce excess cost, while under central privacy, distance representations achieve the optimal rate even in high dimension.

### 565. [LeJEPA Features Are Not Isotropic, and Should Not Be](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31216)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Richeek Das, Pratik Chaudhari  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> The SIGReg term in LeJEPA regularizes features to have an isotropic Gaussian distribution. Our first result is to show that this regularizer, in practice, cannot achieve its goal. We design a modified regularizer to enforce the objective better. This leads to slightly improved performance. For example, on ImageNet-100, our modified regularizer reaches the final accuracy of SIGReg in 1.3$\times$ fewer epochs. Our second result argues that self-supervised encoders should not have isotropic Gaussian features. We show that the alignment term in LeJEPA conflicts with SIGReg, because it couples the magnitude of the features with their directions. In contrast, isotropic Gaussian features would entail independent magnitude and directions. Features obtained with LeJEPA are, in fact, not isotropic. So LeJEPA has two issues. Its regularizer is weak, and the isotropic Gaussian target is too strict. Our third result is about reformulating the isotropic Gaussian regularizer to enforce only the marginal distributions of the magnitude and direction, without enforcing independence. This leads to a simple regularization term, PolarReg, that can be optimized reliably across many different datasets, batch sizes and embedding dimensions without any hyper-parameter changes. On ImageNet-1k, our technique matches the accuracy of LeJEPA and its variants in 4.7$\times$ fewer epochs. It improves the final accuracy by 1.8 points.

### 567. [Self-Supervised Dataset Distillation via Mutual Information Maximization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31225)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Mohammad Hossein Moslemi, Mahdi Beitollahi, Zhimin Mei 等 6 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Dataset distillation (DD) aims to compress a large dataset into a small synthetic one that remains effective for training neural networks. Despite its success in supervised learning, DD remains underexplored in self-supervised learning (SSL), where distilled data must preserve the information needed to learn representations that remain consistent across augmentations. To bridge this gap, we introduce Mutual Information Prototype Alignment (MIPA), an efficient SSL dataset distillation approach that optimizes synthetic images to preserve augmentation-invariant information. Specifically, MIPA treats synthetic images as learnable prototypes and uses a frozen encoder to assign real samples to them based on feature similarity. A synthetic dataset that preserves such augmentation invariance should yield stable prototype assignments across multiple augmented views of the same real image. MIPA encourages this by maximizing mutual information between cross-view assignments while avoiding trivial solutions in which all real samples are assigned to only a few synthetic prototypes. Evaluations on image classification benchmarks show that synthetic data generated by MIPA yield higher-quality representations than existing distillation approaches while reducing time and storage demands during distillation.

### 568. [How Far Can Linear Go? BiPert, a Unified Framework for Single-Cell Perturbation Modelling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31227)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Abhishek Sharma, Aleksej Zelezniak  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Understanding how genetic perturbations affect cellular behaviour is essential for studying gene function and regulatory mechanisms. Large-scale CRISPR screens measure these effects across thousands of genes, but cover relatively few cellular contexts. Predicting perturbation responses in unseen cell types and identifying perturbed genes from their transcriptional effects therefore remain challenging. Here, we show that both tasks can be addressed through a common linear decomposition of transcriptional responses into shared and perturbation-specific components. Building on this principle, we introduce BiPert, a simple linear framework for both forward perturbation prediction and inverse identification. For forward prediction, BiPert outperforms existing methods that use no external biological knowledge when predicting gene expression changes in unseen cell lines. Its few-shot extension, BiPert-FS, adapts to new cell lines using as few as ten measured perturbations. For inverse identification, BiPert-ID identifies perturbed genes from single-cell expression profiles, outperforming deep-learning methods in unseen cell lines and donors. We further show that perturbations whose effects transfer more consistently across cellular contexts are also easier to identify, even after controlling for perturbation effect size. Together, these results establish a connection between perturbation transfer and identification, and demonstrate that simple linear models provide strong, interpretable baselines for both tasks.
