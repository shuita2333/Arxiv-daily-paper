### 51. [Generative Text Modelling and Language-Guided Visual Attention for Deciphering Palimpsests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24060)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Robin Hollifeldt, Tianru Zhang, Jessica Ström 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Recovering obscured text in palimpsests, i.e. the manuscripts where the original writing (under-text) was erased and overwritten for reuse, is vital for preserving lost information. Due to the nature of the material, such a task can be highly challenging and time-consuming. This paper presents an approach towards automated palimpsest handwritten text modeling for under-text recovery. We investigate generative models, including Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and diffusion models, and design a custom denoising diffusion model that learns structured noise patterns to reconstruct the under-text. The recovered text is subsequently processed by a fine-tuned, attention-based handwritten text recognition network. Furthermore, we investigate a language-guided recognition framework that leverages textual class descriptions as informative priors, enabling the text recognition model to distinguish meaningful features from noise. This is achieved by grounding task-specific descriptions, phrased in words or sentences, through visual attention maps generated by pretrained vision-language models like CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training). To facilitate future research and benchmarking, we release new datasets for synthetic palimpsests (currently scarce), CLIP-compatible text descriptions, and pre-trained models with code.

### 52. [From Prefabricated Content to Situated Generation: A Four-Wave Longitudinal Study of AI-Mediated Museum Surfaces and Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24076)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Yibo Meng, Xiujun Chen, Bingyi Liu 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Generative AI changes a basic condition of interactive surfaces and spaces (ISS): visitor-facing content can be produced during interaction rather than fully authored before it. We report a longitudinal qualitative study with 23 Chinese science museum practitioners, interviewed annually from 2023 to 2026 (retention 23 to 19) and analyzed using reflexive thematic analysis. Participants' accounts moved from anticipated epistemic and operational risks, through backstage experimentation and visitor-facing trials, toward situated governance and renewed questions about the purpose of museum interaction. Following these changes over time shows how generative AI reconfigured the design problems of museum ISS: generated content appeared under institutional authority; inference delay became publicly and bodily experienced; concurrent touch, gesture, and speech created ambiguous multi-user intent; and post-generation editing could leave curators selecting within an AI-defined possibility space. We conceptualize the resulting shift as a movement from prefabricated display to situated generation and formulate four design propositions concerning provenance at the point of display, spatially appropriate fallback, multi-intent negotiation, and curatorial control before generation. These propositions are grounded in practitioner accounts rather than evaluations of implemented solutions.

### 53. [OR-License: Evaluating LLM Agents as Real-World Operations Research Practitioners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24087)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Peisong Wang, Feixue Liu, Yize Meng 等 19 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computation and Language

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Can large language model (LLM) agents deliver expert-level operations research (OR) solutions directly from original client materials, without prior expert reformulation? Existing OR benchmarks typically begin with self-contained descriptions or predefined optimization tasks, underrepresenting the translation of original business materials into professionally validated solutions. We introduce OR-License, an end-to-end benchmark of 31 instances across 10 industrial scenarios from paid OR engagements totaling USD 3.68 million. It preserves original requirements, input data, deliverable formats, and information boundaries after de-identification. To study information acquisition, we introduce ORSim, a controlled client simulator that discloses business facts without supplying OR expertise. Hidden evaluation checks feasibility and compares objective quality against original expert solutions accepted by clients before benchmark construction. Deployment-Adjusted Quality (DAQ) averages expert-relative quality across instances, assigning zero to infeasible deliveries. Across 11 frontier LLMs using coding-agent harnesses, several exceed the expert reference in conditional objective quality, but none matches its DAQ; the strongest primary Claude Code configuration achieves 88.39% strict feasibility and a DAQ of 0.87 against the expert reference of 1. Client information acquisition extends beyond clarification into solution development and validation, with some later stages showing higher confirmed issue-resolution proportions. Trajectory analysis identifies formulation and implementation failures, while task-equalized research-chain completion rates range from 24% to 45% across models on evaluable tasks. These findings motivate sustained information-gap resolution, faithful problem formulation, and evidence-linked self-improvement for reliable long-horizon agents.

### 54. [Persistent Memory and Iterative Improvement in an LLM Agent for Chinese-Character Drawing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24090)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Peilin Wu, Yi-Zhuang You  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Artificial Intelligence

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Does persistent memory accumulated from previous attempts help an LLM agent solve new problems, and does memory design affect that benefit? We study programmatic Chinese-character drawing across five agent conditions attempting the same 668 items. Accumulated memory produces modest aggregate gains despite substantial growth in the memory stores: free-form notes improve first-attempt success by 4.6 percentage points, while a design combining free-form memory with reusable code improves it by 2.8 points. The two designs do not differ significantly overall, but the code bank shows a suggestive advantage on later, harder items. A separate post-hoc comparison finds a much larger improvement when the code-bank agent receives privileged, target-specific structural supervision. The agent can therefore exploit highly informative structural guidance, although the privileged per-item information does not establish what could have been learned from experience alone. These results suggest that memory design may affect where accumulated experience becomes useful, even when its average benefit is modest.

### 55. [Hierarchical and Query-Aware Visual Token Pruning for High-Resolution LVLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24102)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Luther Wei, Ling Zou, Kang-Yang Huang 等 5 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> High-resolution Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) preserve critical, fine-grained visual details by encoding a global thumbnail alongside multiple local crops. However, the resulting thousands of vision tokens introduce severe computational bottlenecks during Large Language Model (LLM) inference. Existing token pruning methods rely primarily on query-agnostic visual saliency, while current query-aware approaches either operate at a coarse crop-level resolution or compute token relevance too late, after vision tokens have already entered the heavy LLM layers. To address this, we propose a training-free, hierarchical token pruning framework that utilizes query guidance prior to executing any LLM blocks. Our top-down hierarchy first operates at the instance-level, dynamically scaling the vision token retention ratio of each sample based on its visual complexity. Next, at the crop-level, we distribute this sample budget across local crops using sentence-level cross-modal similarity. Finally, at the token-level, we exploit projected vision tokens alongside textual query token embeddings in the shared space to measure fine-grained alignment, applying a text-debiasing normalization to produce high-fidelity query scores. These query-guided budgets and scores are fused with local and global visual saliency metrics to select the most critical vision tokens. Extensive experiments on LLaVA-NeXT-7B demonstrate that our plug-and-play framework retains 98.0% and 94.8% of unpruned performance at 25% and 10% vision token retention, respectively. Notably, at 25% retention, our method delivers a 2.0x inference throughput speedup and even surpasses the unpruned baseline on POPE by 0.7 points.

### 56. [Cache2LoRA: Understanding and Improving Context Internalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24107)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Yi Fang, Gaurav Srivastava, Zhenyu Bi 等 5 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Artificial Intelligence

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Context-to-LoRA methods encode documents as low-rank parameter updates, allowing language models to answer without retaining the source text in their decoding context. How reliably do these updates preserve facts and support their use under different instructions? Controlled diagnosis of existing context-to-LoRA methods separates fact discrimination, target retention, and response adaptation. Competing sources influence answers, while target-only construction and memory-state optimization improve recovery at fixed update size. Recovering facts also does not ensure that answers follow response instructions. We introduce Cache2LoRA, which uses each query to aggregate evidence from a reusable frozen document cache before generating LoRA updates. Trainable encoding operates only on the query and memory-token suffix, and multi-style supervision teaches both evidence preservation and answer expression. Across 17 tasks, Cache2LoRA improves on existing context-to-LoRA methods and achieves macro F1 of 0.663, approaching full-context in-context learning at 0.671. For 32K documents and 256-token outputs at batch size 16, its decoding throughput is 5.9$\times$ that of full-context ICL. At matched memory size, generated LoRA preserves answer accuracy more reliably than the learned-KV variant under long, diverse intervening text and combines more effectively with retained document KV, yielding higher hybrid F1 and EM.

### 57. [Joint, Decoupled, or Agent: When Each Multimodal Alignment Paradigm Wins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24108)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Yi Fang, Gaurav Srivastava, Sirui Ding 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Artificial Intelligence

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Jointly pretrained multimodal encoders are a common way to combine modalities, while alternatives integrate their information during downstream training or inference. When does each approach win? We compare three paradigms, instantiated with public checkpoints, across 16 paired datasets in five domains and multiple training-label budgets. JOINT trains a fusion head on frozen, jointly pretrained encoders. DECOUPLED trains a fusion head on frozen, independently pretrained encoders. AGENT gives a frozen LLM task instructions, labelled examples, available sample text, and supervised unimodal readouts. No paradigm dominates the complete 144 dataset-budget combinations: DECOUPLED wins 54, JOINT 44, and AGENT 46 by mean utility. ScienceQA, MELD, and MetMeme shift from AGENT to fusion as supervision increases. Between the fusion paradigms, JOINT leads on four of five QA datasets across all evaluated budgets, while higher unimodal 5-NN accuracy is associated with a relative shift toward DECOUPLED across eight classification datasets (Spearman $\rho=-0.67$). We test whether representation and task diagnostics predict these preferences on held-out datasets before fitting their candidate systems. A two-step cascade combines these diagnostics with task and budget metadata to select AGENT or fusion, then JOINT or DECOUPLED. Under leave-one-group-out evaluation, it selects the winner in 71.4% of cells versus 54.2% for metadata-only routing, with mean relative regret 0.028 versus 0.057. Using geometry, supervised representation diagnostics, and text-label matching together yields higher accuracy and lower regret than using any one of these sources with metadata. These results connect paradigm preferences to supervision budget, task format, and unimodal 5-NN accuracy. Target-task diagnostics thus support selecting among the three paradigms before candidate fitting.

### 58. [Do LMs Know What Variables Do? Interpreting Code Representations via Variable Roles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24118)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Célian Vasson, Daniil Ognev, Kentaro Inui 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computation and Language

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Variable roles describe recurring behaviors such as accumulation, traversal, and retaining the best candidate encountered. We use Sajaniemi's independently defined taxonomy to study which role distinctions can be recovered from large language models, how sparse autoencoders (SAEs) retain those distinctions, and whether associated directions support control of generation. We introduce static annotation rules for all eleven roles and a corpus of 126,926 natural-code files across five languages. Across five model-SAE systems, residual probes outperform individual SAE features and tested lexical baselines, with modest losses after neutral target renaming. Combining SAE features closes much of the single-feature gap, but probes on SAE reconstructions are less sensitive to role-changing edits reviewed by LLMs. High paired ranking accuracy coexists with frequent false positives at fixed thresholds. During generation, interventions often change role annotations at the cost of correctness; changes accompanied by passing tests show no clear aggregate advantage over random controls. The benchmark separates role-related recoverability, SAE fidelity, and correctness-preserving control through matched prediction, source edits, and generation interventions.

### 59. [Prefix-Tuning for Arbitrary Output Sequences on Pretrained Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24123)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Peter Cho-Ho Lam, Ziyi Wang, Zirui Zhou  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computation and Language

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Prefix-tuning is a parameter-efficient fine-tuning method for large language models (LLMs) that steers model outputs by prepending task-specific prefix representations to the input sequence. Despite its practical success, the theoretical understanding of the expressiveness of prefixes remains incomplete. In particular, the following question remains open: Given a pretrained autoregressive Transformer, for any input-output sequence pair $(\mathtt{x},\mathtt{y})$, does there always exist a prefix $\mathcal{S}$ such that prepending $\mathcal{S}$ to $\mathtt{x}$ induces the model to generate the target output $\mathtt{y}$? In this work, we prove that under the assumption that $\mathtt{y}$ is in the range of the pretrained Transformer, such a prefix $\mathcal{S}$ exists and can be explicitly computed by solving multiple linear systems. We further show that this assumption is necessary, in the sense that such a prefix may not exist when the assumption fails, thereby providing a complete answer to the above question. Our analysis is constructive and the obtained theoretical results hold under fairly general assumptions on model architecture. Extensive experiments on GPT-2 and Qwen3-4B demonstrate that our algorithm produces prefixes with strong control over model outputs.

### 60. [The Weight of Teamwork: Does Communication Structure Help LLM Scientists Search for Scientific Laws?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24131)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Jianing Shi, Zaibin Zhang, Shijun Lei 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Multiagent Systems

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Team performance is often reported as one delivered score, even though that score is produced in two stages: a system generates candidate answers and a selection rule returns one of them. We use symbolic regression as an observable scientific-search setting and compare fixed-budget LLM teams that differ in peer visibility and update timing, evaluating both their frozen candidate archives and the expressions selected from those archives. Distributing the call budget across more independent trajectories produced the largest measured gain, improving mean held-out fitting score Q by 0.70 at the widest tested allocation. Under a common archive-wide selector, the implemented Ring policy reduced mean Q by 0.33 relative to Independent, while Star and Complete remained unresolved. Holding the generated archives fixed, changing the delivery rule changed the measured architecture gaps, including the sign of the Complete point estimate. These results support reporting candidate generation and final selection as separate endpoints. They do not identify a communication mechanism or a universally preferred topology; rather, they show that an architecture ranking is not identified by communication structure alone when the rule that returns the final answer is left unspecified.

### 61. [A Conditional No-Hair Theorem for Transformers: How Language Models Forget Their Prompt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24133)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Shuyang Xiang  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Artificial Intelligence

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> We ask how much an autoregressive transformer retains of its prompt after generating $t$ tokens. Consider two different prompts followed by the same continuation text, so that any difference in their hidden states comes from the prompt alone, and we find that the distance between their hidden states decays approximately as a power law $t^{-\alpha}$, where $\alpha=1$ is the rate at which a fixed prompt's share of uniform attention shrinks and smaller values mean longer memory. Trained models forget fast ($\alpha\approx0.55$--$0.7$), untrained ones slowly ($\alpha\approx0.2$--$0.5$). We prove that this offset is $O\big(P(\log t)^{L-1}/t\big)$ for a prompt of length $P$ and depth $L$. The bound holds for finite-depth pre-LN softmax transformers with bounded attention logits, under one additional condition: the pre-norm activations stay away from zero. The mechanism is geometric: the prompt's direct influence is diluted as the context grows, and \emph{relay} --- continuation tokens passing on what earlier tokens read from the prompt --- can only add logarithmic factors. This is what we call the \emph{conditional no-hair theorem}: given the text generated so far, the prompt's influence on the next token vanishes as $t$ grows, so any property of the prompt that survives, such as its language, must be carried by the text itself. An exactly solvable linear relay model shows that the bound cannot be improved and reproduces the rise of the local exponent towards $1$ seen in untrained networks. Cancelling the dilution with a logit bias stops the decay early in training and reverses it after, so dilution is necessary for the forgetting we measure; untrained networks and the solvable model show it is also sufficient.

### 62. [Continual Test-Time Learning with Evolution Strategies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24137)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Guangya Hao, Yiting Duan, Longyan Tan 等 8 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Large language models increasingly operate in open-ended environments, where they are expected to continually adapt to evolving tasks and distributions, often without access to external supervision. Existing continual learning approaches rely on external supervision, such as labeled data, demonstrations, or reward signals, which can be costly or unavailable in practice. Test-time adaptation removes this dependence by learning from self-generated supervision, but existing methods derive that supervision from the single policy being updated, so once the policy drifts, its own learning signal drifts with it, leading to policy collapse, degraded reasoning, and catastrophic forgetting. In this paper, we propose \textbf{Evolution Strategy Reinforcement Learning (ESRL)}, a continual test-time learning framework that draws its self-generated supervision from a population of policies instead of the single policy being trained. ESRL perturbs an anchor policy in parameter space to form a population of nearby policies, samples one trajectory from each, and aggregates their answers into label-free group-relative learning signals for the central student. The anchor is held fixed within each block and refreshed from the student at block boundaries, so the population stays centered on a stable yet current policy and continues to supply diverse and reliable supervision as the student moves. In single-task test-time adaptation across mathematical reasoning, general knowledge, and medical domains, ESRL improves average accuracy by up to 31.7% relative to the base model and 16.6% relative to the strongest test-time adaptation baseline. Notably, ESRL substantially reduces catastrophic forgetting and achieves the strongest final performance in continual learning.

### 63. [CoDA: A Human-LM Collaborative Codebook Development Assistant for Domain-Specific Text Annotation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24157)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Jaspreet Ranjit, Swaminathan Chellappa, Augusto Rivas Costante 等 8 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computation and Language

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> We present CoDA, an open-source human-LM collaborative codebook development assistant that helps domain experts develop annotation guidelines for annotating new variables in unstructured text with language models. Existing language model annotation systems primarily focus on downstream classification performance, while assuming the availability of high-quality annotation guidelines, or codebooks. In practice, developing these codebooks is time-consuming and challenging, particularly for concepts that require expert knowledge. CoDA addresses this gap by collaborating with domain experts to iteratively develop codebooks through targeted review of incorrect language model predictions and incorporating expert feedback into the evolving codebook. We demonstrate CoDA on two real-world case studies: extracting health indicators from homelessness case management notes and classifying sentiment of wildlife trafficking posts on social media. Applying CoDA to generate annotations at scale, we identified 2,410 case notes that contained health discussions or referrals and 10,196 social media posts expressing negative sentiment that may warrant investigation for potential wildlife trafficking. By accelerating codebook development, CoDA helps practitioners use language models to extract domain-specific insights from unstructured text at scale.

### 64. [Automated Rule Mutation for Emerging Attack Variants using Infrastructure-Aware Technique Proximity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24162)

**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Cryptography and Security

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Detection rules are typically crafted based on specific observations of an attack. While these rules are effective for particular attack instances, their coverage diminishes when adversaries modify their techniques, such as by employing alternative execution paths or adapting to different telemetry environments while achieving the same objective. To address this challenge, we introduce ARMS, an automated rule mutation solution that generates variant detection rules. ARMS defines a novel three-dimensional proximity space to identify techniques related to the original attack that capture structural relations, functional similarity, and environmental constraints. We further evaluated ARMS across multiple research questions, showing the effectiveness of each proximity dimension and infrastructure-aware rule customization. Nearly all generated variant rules successfully compiled into SIEM queries, and ~92% of the newly generated rules detect the original behavior despite attackers' technique variations.

### 66. [Model Capacity Frontier: A Development Framework for LLM-Supported Social Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24168)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Patrick Zhao, Yuxuan Li, Hirokazu Shirado  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Multiagent Systems

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Large language model (LLM) agents are increasingly incorporated into agent-based models (ABMs) on the premise that richer, context-sensitive human behavior can improve empirical fidelity. However, LLM-supported ABMs introduce an open-ended design space in which subtle modeling and configuration choices, such as prompt design and environment coupling, can substantially alter outcomes. Developing LLM-supported ABMs to simulate human collective behavior therefore requires navigating configuration sensitivity while identifying the limitations of the current model architecture. In this paper, we introduce the \textit{Model Capacity Frontier} (MCF), a framework that characterizes the empirical performance attainable by a specified architecture across its admissible configurations. In multi-objective settings, this capacity is represented by an estimated Pareto frontier obtained through systematic configuration exploration. We demonstrate MCF in crowd simulation of university commencement events with approximately 12,000 agents, using empirical movement data on temporal occupancy, spatial distribution, and flow direction. We first characterize the capacity of a conventional crowd model and then evaluate successive LLM-supported architectural extensions. In the process, MCF reveals which dimensions of attainable performance expand and which remain limited, providing empirical targets for refinement and re-evaluation. Initial LLM integration improves flow direction but degrades temporal occupancy and spatial distribution; subsequent architectural refinement of the LLM layer improves temporal occupancy and recovers spatial performance, while retaining much of the earlier gain in flow direction. Our case study shows how MCF guides the systematic architectural development of LLM-supported ABMs in configuration-sensitive, open-ended design spaces.

### 67. [Prosodic Structure as a Critical Component in Phonemic Language Modelling at BabyLM Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24172)

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Human children learn language from continuous, prosodically structured speech long before they can read, yet almost all language models train on orthographic text. Building on previous phonemic BabyLM training (Goriely et al., 2024; arXiv:2410.22906), we ask whether supplying an estimate of the prosody infants exploit improves phonemic language modelling at BabyLM scale. We compare an orthographic baseline to three conditions forming a ladder of phonological richness. All conditions are trained as BERT MLMs at 10M and 100M words. Phonemic models with boundary structure are competitive with the orthographic baseline. These findings suggest that prosodic structure is a key component of phonemic language modelling in the small-data regime most analogous to infant learning.

### 69. [Reasoning with Context Management and External Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24187)
