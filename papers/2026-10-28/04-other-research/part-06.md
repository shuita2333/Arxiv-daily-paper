### 251. [Regional prediction of hematoma expansion from non-contrast computed tomography in spontaneous intracerebral hemorrhage patients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34645)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Natasha Ironside, Hongyu Kan, Kristofor Pas 等 20 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Background The combination of hematoma location, expansion and final volume influence neurological outcome. The aim of this study was to derive and validate a quantitative and interpretable region-based NCCT image feature model for predicting hematoma expansion. Methods The Ethnic/Racial Variations of Intracerebral Hemorrhage(ERICH) study was used for derivation and the Virtual International Trials in Stroke Archive(VISTA) was used for validation. NCCT images at presentation and 24+/-6hours were pre-processed, segmented and categorized into lobar, basal ganglia or thalamic regions. Hematoma expansion was defined by region and according to its effect on clinical outcome. Transport-based morphometry (TBM) produced a unique transport-based representation from each NCCT hematoma image. Linear classifiers applied to transport representations were used to derive a predictive model. The regional TBM model's performance was compared to alternate methods using AUROC analyses. Results The derivation dataset included 574 patients, comprising 162 lobar, 257 basal ganglia and 155 thalamic hematomas. The validation dataset included 196 patients, comprising 31 lobar, 119 basal ganglia and 46 thalamic hematomas. A combined regional model predicted hematoma expansion in the validation dataset with an AUROC of 0.787. The principal image features which varied by location were size, heterogeneity, eccentricity and density distribution towards the hematoma periphery. The regional TBM model outperformed alternative clinician based and machine-learning methods. Conclusions TBM discriminated and visualized regional features of hematoma expansion from presentation NCCT scans. This provides preliminary evidence for regional radiographic features of hematoma expansion. It has the potential to improve hematoma expansion prediction and understanding of hematoma expansion pathophysiology.

### 252. [Rewriting the Self: User Perceptions of AI-Mediated Cognitive Reframing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34647)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Yuewen Yang, Olzhas Yessenbayev, Anna R. Van Meter 等 5 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Individuals are increasingly using AI for psychological support. One specific paradigm, AI-mediated cognitive reframing (AIM-CR), uses AI to automatically reframe users' negative thoughts ("I failed...") into more adaptive perspectives ("I am learning..."). While prior work suggests that AIM-CR tools reduce users' negative emotions, the complexities of AI automatically modifying users' personal narratives remain underexplored. In this work, we investigated these complexities through a mixed-methods study in which 28 participants engaged with an AIM-CR tool that reframed narratives about their stressors. Our findings suggest that participants had mixed perceptions of AIM-CR: although reframing reduced negative emotions for most participants, participants felt less ownership of, and less authenticity in, the reframed narratives. Participants perceived AIM-CR as a validating "mirror," as "another self" that could motivate or alienate them, or as an external social actor. These perceptions shaped therapeutic dynamics. We conclude by discussing design implications for future AIM-CR tools to better preserve user agency in narrative co-construction and more effectively support mental health and well-being.

### 253. [The CAPED Framework: Characterizing User Skills in Chart Authoring through the Lens of Tasks and Tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34652)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Bongshin Lee, Zhicheng Liu, John Thompson 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Human-Computer Interaction

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> In this paper, we introduce a new framework for characterizing the user skills required for chart authoring. Despite growing attention to authoring systems, we lack a conceptual framework that characterizes the diverse and multifaceted skills involved and helps identify opportunities for system design and development. Our initial investigation revealed that skill is a complex and elusive concept, and that existing theories on skills from other disciplines are too high-level to capture the importance of tasks and tools in data visualizations. To address this gap, we formulate the CAPED framework that characterizes skill as the ability to create a chart involving five dimensions pertinent to tasks and tools: input data, output chart, paradigm, abstraction, and expression. We demonstrate the framework's descriptive power by highlighting different skills involved in similar authoring situations and identifying the types of knowledge associated with chart authoring. We further present examples illustrating how the framework can be used to identify new research opportunities in supporting users with varying skills.

### 255. [Hierarchical Task Network planning in the Goal Lifecycle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34668)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Paola Rizzo, Hector Munoz-Avila, David W. Aha  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Artificial Intelligence

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Goal reasoning (GR) is the process by which intelligent agents continually reason about the goals they are pursuing, which may lead to goal change, for instance when opportunities arise, or when events cause discrepancies among expected and actual outcomes of actions planned to achieve goals. In this paper we investigate whether Hierarchical Task Network (HTN) planning can provide a uniform implementation of the Goal Lifecycle, a flexible process model for goal reasoning in dynamic environments. The Goal Lifecycle progressively refines goals by adding constraints to them through a series of strategies (e.g., {\it formulate, select, expand}) and uses other strategies (e.g., {\it defer, re-expand, repair}) to resolve the current goal when an event impacts it. We present a novel GR agent architecture that employs HTN planning in this Goal Lifecycle. In addition to using HTN planning for the \textit{expand} and \textit{re-expand} strategies, as is frequently done in GR and planning research, we also use it in the \textit{formulate} strategy, to obtain a uniform planning approach that provides several benefits (e.g., the possibility to formulate new goals in view of achieving higher level overarching goals). We also explain how we use an enhanced HTN action representation to process discrepancies among expected and actual action outcomes that can trigger the transition from one goal to another. Finally, we empirically demonstrate that our GR architecture enables simulated autonomous vehicles to adaptively conduct missions in dynamic environments.

### 256. [Compound Environmental Operating Stress and Cyber-Induced Pump Disruption in Water Distribution Networks: Matched Hydraulic Impacts, Service Continuity, and Sustained Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34670)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Halil Dursunoglu, Fatma Dursunoglu  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Cryptography and Security

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Digitally controlled water distribution systems must maintain service during disruptions, yet restoring water delivery does not necessarily ensure hydraulic recovery. This study evaluates cyber-induced pump outages under hypothetical environmental operating stress using two EPANET benchmark networks (Net1 and Net3) simulated with the Water Network Tool for Resilience. The experimental design comprises 288 simulation records (240 distinct configurations), including 192 verified attack scenarios, with four- and twelve-hour outages, six hydraulic configurations, and four mitigation strategies. All attack scenarios satisfied pump-shutdown verification and pre-attack hydraulic-equivalence criteria. Twelve-hour outages produced an average of 404.96 m$^3$ of unmet demand in Net1, while Net3 maintained full modeled demand delivery despite pressure deviations. Sustained pressure recovery occurred in 103 of 192 scenarios, with 65 exhibiting subsequent relapse, whereas all scenarios satisfied the post-outage water-service recovery criterion. Combined mitigation eliminated unmet demand in evaluated Net1 scenarios, while outage shortening achieved the highest sustained-pressure-recovery count. These findings highlight network-dependent resilience and the distinction between service restoration and hydraulic recovery. Environmental stress scenarios represent sensitivity assumptions rather than empirically calibrated climate impacts.

### 257. [Exploring perceptual straightness in learned visual representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34678)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Anne Harrington, Vasha DuTell, Ayush Tewari 等 7 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Humans have been shown to use a "straightened" encoding to represent the natural visual world as it evolves in time (Hénaff et al. 2019). In the context of discrete video sequences, "straightened" means that changes between frames follow a more linear path in representation space at progressively deeper levels of processing. While deep convolutional networks are often proposed as models of human visual processing, many do not straighten natural videos. In this paper, we explore the relationship between network architecture, differing types of robustness, biologically-inspired filtering mechanisms, and representational straightness in response to time-varying input; we identify strengths and limitations of straightness as a useful way of evaluating neural network representations. We find that (1) adversarial training leads to straighter representations in both CNN and transformer-based architectures but (2) this effect is task-dependent, not generalizing to tasks such as segmentation and frame-prediction, where straight representations are not favorable for predictions; and nor to other types of robustness. In addition, (3) straighter representations impart temporal stability to class predictions, even for out-of-distribution data. Finally, (4) biologically-inspired elements increase straightness in the early stages of a network, but do not guarantee increased straightness in downstream layers of CNNs. We show that straightness is an easily computed measure of representational robustness and stability, as well as a hallmark of human representations with benefits for computer vision models.

### 258. [Beyond Deduplication: Sharp Retrieval Limits for Attention with Correlated Distractors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34682)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Heehyun Park  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Redundant context affects attention through two distinct mechanisms: repeated items add normalization mass, while score variation within correlated groups can produce extreme individual scores. We separate these effects in a planted retrieval model with exponentially many Gaussian distractors arranged in known, exponentially large clusters. The model is a two-level generalized random energy model, whose classical free energy yields exact retrieval thresholds. Gaussian interpolation further shows that increasing positive correlation at fixed token count lowers the distractor free energy, unlike adding duplicates. Our main result identifies a sharp limitation of subtracting log cluster size from pre-softmax scores: this correction removes multiplicity but retains within-cluster extremes. Even a temperature chosen after observing all scores cannot lower the required signal below $\sqrt{2\alpha_1}$, where $\alpha_1$ is the exponential rate of the number of clusters. Averaging scores before exponentiation instead suppresses these fluctuations and achieves $\sqrt{2\rho\alpha_1}$, where $\rho$ is the within-cluster correlation. The gap persists after adding exponentially many singleton distractors, so group size alone does not identify the target, and extends to fixed finite hierarchies of light-tailed independent-increment scores. Finite-sample, stability, and participation results complement the asymptotic analysis. A controlled retrieval experiment with frozen sentence embeddings supports this distinction while delineating its scope: averaging helps when the relevant item is a singleton, but can discard useful evidence when a relevant group contains multiple matching items.

Its clearest joint result is on KLIFS, combining reduced kinase-group recoverability, strong structural prediction, and valid rigid poses.

### 261. [Stable Continuous-Time Consistency Distillation: An Empirical Study with a Multistep Extension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34704)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Vinay Saji Mathew, Soundar R. Kumara, Gretta D Kellogg 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Continuous-time consistency models are an attractive route to few-step generation. They remove the discretization error inherent to the timestep grid that discrete-time consistency models depend on. Until recently, however, training them was unstable. Lu & Song (2025) address this with the TrigFlow formulation and a coordinated set of architectural and training-objective changes. We conduct an independent empirical study of this framework on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet-64. No open-weight TrigFlow teachers exist at these scales, so we train them from scratch and then verify the consistency distillation (sCD) and consistency training (sCT) behavior of students initialized from these teachers. On CIFAR-10, our students reproduce the reported FID at one and two steps. On ImageNet-64, they fall short, a gap that appears related to our teacher and to architectural choices the paper leaves unspecified. We also report a parameter-count gap of about 5% between the model size stated in the paper and a faithful re-implementation, which we attribute to the scale-and-shift conditioning projections in Adaptive Double Normalization. We then propose Multistep sCD (MS-sCD), a continuous-time, segment-conditioned multistep generalization of sCD. The segment-conditioning idea is borrowed from the discrete-time multistep consistency distillation of Heek et al. (2024); we build on their preliminary continuous-time experiments within the TrigFlow/sCM framework. The rest of the algorithm follows sCD. MS-sCD replaces sCM's two-step intermediate time with a fixed segment-boundary schedule and recovers sCD at M = 1. We report FID at M = 2, 4, 8 and compare against the discrete-time MSCD baseline. Code, model weights, and training scripts are available on GitHub (Mathew et al., 2026).

However, most AI harm taxonomies lack child-specific evidence and under-specify developmental harm and the stakeholder asymmetries unique to minors. Building on prior taxonomies, we construct a child-centered harm taxonomy mapped to sociotechnical failure modes. ### 263. [Sparse Reconstruction by Direct Measurement-to-Field Generation in Function Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34709)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Linzheng Wang, Jason Chen, Nicolas Tricard 等 6 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Understanding physical systems often requires recovering their full spatial state from measurements available only at sparse locations. This reconstruction becomes challenging when physical boundaries are unknown, high-resolution training fields are unavailable, or important quantities cannot be measured directly. Sparse observations may also be consistent with multiple plausible states, leading to significant reconstruction uncertainty. Here we introduce Direct Measurement-to-Field Generation (\DMF{}), a functional rectified flow framework that learns how sparse measurements drive physical-field generation end to end. Instead of relying on grid-based rasterization or runtime guidance, \DMF{} jointly learns a sensor--global--sensor representation with the generative transport, dynamically fusing local grounding with global context to evaluate physical fields at arbitrary query coordinates. Across benchmarks involving unobserved boundaries, missing high-resolution training fields, and unmeasured physical quantities, \DMF{} outperforms alternative baselines by significantly reducing reconstruction errors and preserving the fidelity of physical properties. Furthermore, its stochastic ensemble generation reliably quantifies uncertainty while improving continuous ranked probability scores. These results extend sparse reconstruction from fixed-setting point estimation toward measurement-conditioned generation of physical fields under incomplete and changing information.

### 264. [Intervene, Calibrate, Discover: Learning Causal Structure from Pixels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34711)

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Learning high-level causal variables from low-level observations is a central problem for artificial intelligence. With known node semantics but an unknown graph, visual readout errors can misalign model settings with environment intervention values. We address this mismatch by pairing image readouts with verified values attained through fixed round-robin interventions. With encoder and decoder frozen, a dimension-wise invertible calibration map puts visual responses and target settings in shared numerical units. Controlled domino experiments show that interventions resolve orientation ambiguity left by equal additional observations. In a matched-data domino control, calibration reduces the mean error of predicted changes in other variables within the same factual context, relative to an uncalibrated model.

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**

We introduce Coordinator-Executor Prompt Optimization (CEPO), a general framework for improving prompt optimization in MAS. CEPO separates system-level coordination from agent-level execution through a shared coordinator prompt and agent-specific executor prompts, and guides their updates according to their respective roles.
