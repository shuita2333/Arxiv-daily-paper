### 1. [Discourse Analysis of Legislative Bill Discussions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05381)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Isaac Rudnick, Nipun Das, Foaad Khosmood  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computation and Language

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Legislative hearings are distinct communication events with their own human recognizable structure and logic, yet they have been analyzed as distinct phenomena beyond their transcripts. Understanding legislative hearings at a deeper level is a major step in analyzing the work and power relationships within democratic bodies. In this work, we present a novel end-to-end pipeline for parsing rhetorical structures from legislative committee hearings with the goal of improving legislative discourse analysis and democratic accountability. We also propose a rhetorical structure grammar and find that 55.21% of California state bill discussions in our dataset can be parsed with this grammar. We evaluate our pipeline with two utterance section classifiers on a random subset of over 2,000 annotated utterances corresponding to 100 hearings. We find that a logistic regression-based classifier with grammar-based output masking and utterance tag smoothing outperforms more complex models, achieving an F1 score of 52.8% against our ground truth discourse section labels.

### 2. [Human-like cross-language generalisation in deep neural speaker embeddings and its acoustic foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05384)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Tianze Xu, Xiyang Li, Xiaoming Jiang 等 4 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computation and Language

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Generalisability is a central question for deep neural networks, including the cross-language generalisation of speaker embeddings. This study examined whether deep neural speaker embeddings generalise across languages and how such generalisation relates to human perception and acoustic structure. Using Cantonese--English bilingual speech presented in within- and cross-language conditions, we derived similarity from 18 state-of-the-art models and compared it with human ratings and acoustic features using linear mixed models and representational similarity analysis. Embeddings exhibited cross-language generalisation that broadly paralleled human behaviour, reflecting accurate identity discrimination and sensitivity to language switching. This generalisation relied on largely shared acoustic cues with language-sensitive weighting, mirroring human patterns. Together, these findings provide converging evidence for human--machine alignment in cross-language voice similarity processing.

### 3. [Same Origin, Different Verdicts: Text Production Conditions Confound Machine-Generated Text Detection in Russian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05385)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Stanislav Kirichenko  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computation and Language

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Automated detection of machine-generated text is applied where the verdict carries a cost for the author, yet origin is a property of the production process, not an observable property of the text. The conditions under which a text is produced push it in the same direction in which a generative model is presumed to push it. This study measures the contribution of these conditions while origin is held fixed. The Russian-language corpus of 1882 documents comprises a machine part, generated by four generation channels (each backed by its own model family) across 45 tasks in three task modes, and an archival human part annotated for source regimentation level before the texts were read. The primary contrast is computed within the machine corpus: both halves of a pair are generated by the same model for the same task and differ only in task wording. Machine-likeness is measured by four operationalisations: an interpretable index with weights fixed before data collection, a trained classifier, a zero-shot metric on an open model, a judge model. The main result is disagreement between procedures: changing the task mode shifted the scores of all four, but three gave a negative shift, the judge a positive one, and none of the four intervals covers zero. The comparison plan and the specifications of procedures 2-4 were fixed after the result of procedure 1, before procedures 2-4 ran; the disagreement survived correction of a corpus defect and of the nested validation. A baseline on the source identifier alone reaches a median AUROC of 1.0000 on the thirteen group splits where it is defined, and removing emphasis markers flipped a confident machine verdict to human without changing the full text by a single character. The scores are properties of a specific measurement system and corpus: they reflect data collection, repetition, formatting, and preprocessing decisions as well as linguistic style.

### 4. [Visual Forensic Detection of Fabricated Bangla News Photocards using Deep Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05403)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Anindya Kundu, Kabya Mithun Saha, Md. Muhaiminul Islam 等 6 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> In Bangladesh, much localized misinformation spreads as photocards: single-image news summaries that imitate the branding of well-known news organizations. Because the misleading message lives in the pixels rather than in machine-readable text, text-based fact-checking tools cannot see it. We recast photocard fake-news detection as visual image forgery detection. We build a dataset of 969 authentic and 1,077 fabricated Bangla photocards scraped from public Facebook pages: authentic cards from the verified pages of seventeen national television broadcasters, fabricated ones from pseudo-broadcaster pages, anonymous profiles, and public groups. Sixteen photometric and morphological transformations per image grow the corpus to 32,736 samples. A fully connected ANN reaches only about 52% accuracy, its loss stuck at ln(2) ~ 0.693, showing that flattening the image destroys the spatial cues forgery detection needs. A compact, fully batch-normalized four-block CNN with 489,954 parameters, about 1,644x smaller than the ANN, is evaluated with leakage-free stratified group five-fold cross-validation, which keeps every augmented version of a card in one fold. Under this protocol the CNN reaches 87.45% +/- 0.53% pooled accuracy from scratch and 88.75% +/- 1.55% when its classifier head is warm-started from the ANN. An ImageNet-pretrained YOLOv8n-cls backbone (1.44M parameters) fine-tuned on the same folds reaches 97.36% +/- 0.42%, marking the headroom above the compact model. We report per-class precision, recall, F1, and pooled confusion matrices, diagnose and fix a one-class collapse caused by an unnormalized classifier head, and give a manual error analysis and bias-variance diagnosis showing that the compact CNNs under-fit.

### 5. [mmHand: Toward Pixel-Level-Accuracy Hand Localization Using a Single Commodity mmWave Device](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05404)

The task involves modeling the hand as a singular point and determining its position within a defined coordinate system. However, due to data modality limitations, existing hand localization technologies face several challenges. For example, vision-based localization raises privacy concerns, while wearable-based methods compromise user comfort. In this paper, we introduce mmHand, a new device-free, privacy-preserving dynamic hand localization system with pixel-level accuracy, using a single commodity mmWave device. Finally, we present a cross-modality spatial feature-enhanced model with high spatial feature extraction capabilities, which can accurately localize hand positions at the pixel level in the mmWave radar U-V pixel coordinate system. We evaluate the system with experiments on 12 subjects in three scenarios, and the results across four metrics demonstrate the effectiveness of our hand localization system.

### 6. [When Does Sharing Work? Scaling a Shared SigLIP2 Across Image, Video, Audio, and Audio-Visual Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05406)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Xiaofei Sun, Peng Wang, Huayao Liu 等 7 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Contrastive pretraining has advanced image-text understanding, yet image, video, and audio encoders are still trained independently, with cross-modal alignment realized mainly through post-hoc binding to a frozen anchor space. We present Omni-Encoder, a general multimodal encoder that processes image, video, audio, and audio-visual inputs within a single SigLIP-2 Vision Transformer with fully shared parameters. Unlike anchor-centric binding or per-modality adapters, all modalities are optimized jointly end-to-end, and the framework is organized from input to training. A modality-native input stage first maps each signal into the shared embedding space, including a 1D-convolutional audio tokenizer that converts mel-spectrograms into tokens aligned with visual patches. A multimodal rotary position embedding parameterized by absolute timestamps then places visual frames and audio segments in a unified spatiotemporal coordinate system. Finally, alternating-modality training interleaves image, video, audio, and temporally aligned audio-visual batches and accumulates their gradients into one update, which we find central to stable joint optimization. Omni-Encoder achieves competitive results on 16 benchmarks across all four modalities with one shared parameter set, and joint training improves each modality over its separately trained counterpart. Code and models will be publicly released.

### 7. [PEEK: One-Step-Look-Ahead Exposure Bias Correction for Diffusion Sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05407)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Yunwei Bai, Ying Kiat Tan, Tsuhan Chen  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Diffusion image generators are successful due to accurate denoising networks and strong numerical samplers. Yet the two components are not fully matched. During sampling, the denoiser predicts for the next step based on flawed input that differ much from the real data. This train-test gap, or exposure bias, caps image generation quality (i.e., blurry, lacking in details or awkward in shape). To mitigate the problem, we propose PEEK, a novel training-free deterministic sampler plugin. At each eligible denoising step, PEEK first performs a provisional one-step update, evaluates the denoiser once at the look-ahead state, and interpolates it with the one-step-later prediction to form the next noisy state. By taking the additional step, we obtain a generally more-image-like prediction from the higher-signal noisy state, mitigating the exposure bias. To optimize the interpolation positions, PEEK searches for an interpolation schedule search made efficient by the prefix and baseline noise-schedule-rebasing caches. On 8 pixel-space and latent-space diffusion models with unconditional, label-guided, and text-guided settings across various output resolutions, PEEK consistently improves over NFE-matched baselines, with FID improvements up to 49.3\% at minimal extra computation cost (e.g., the one-time-per-model calibration takes at most 2.2 minutes across our experiments.). Project website is at this https URL.

### 8. [Augmentation with Reinforcement Transformation for Low-textured Scientific Sketch Image Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05408)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Zhiqiang Wang, Sheri L. Barton, Jennifer H. Johnson 等 5 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Low-textured sketch images are widely used in scientific applications, including wildlife conservation, archaeological illustration, and forensic composites. Learning from and retrieving such images is challenging because they lack detailed surface textures and visual variations commonly exploited by modern deep learning models. This paper proposes Augmentation with Reinforcement Transformation (ART) to fine-tune pretrained models to learn effective representations for accurate sketch image retrieval. Our key observation is that sketches are particularly sensitive to image transformations, such as rotation, flipping, zooming, and stretching. While some transformations preserve their structural integrity, others may introduce distortions that hinder representation learning. Instead of randomly choosing transformations to construct fine-tuning views, ART formulates augmentation selection as a multi-armed bandit (MAB) problem, where transformations are treated as arms and their effectiveness is continuously evaluated through rewards. This enables ART to adaptively construct reward-guided view banks before self-supervised fine-tuning; the subsequent representation loss does not feed back to ART. On Florida manatee sketches provided by Mote Marine Laboratory, ART achieves 71.40% average Top-1 accuracy for transformed-scar retrieval, compared with 68.83% for random augmentation selection, and 55.84% for location-aware sketch retrieval, compared with 18.18% for frozen CLIP.

### 9. [PointNDF: Cascaded Neural Displacement Fields for Point Cloud Denoising](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05411)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Michael Y. Wang, Pingping Cai  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Point cloud denoising aims to recover clean geometry from noisy 3D observations while preserving both fine surface details and the underlying spatial distribution of the points. Many learning-based methods adopt a two-stage pipeline that first extracts point-wise features from the noisy input and subsequently predicts point-wise displacements via simple regressions. However, these approaches often lack robust feature guidance and distribution-aware geometric refinement, causing them to fail at maintaining optimal point uniformity and geometric quality. To address these limitations, we propose PointNDF, a denoising framework that combines patch-level geometric context with local displacement prediction. Specifically, a Point Transformer V3 (PTv3) encoder aggregates multiscale geometric information into a global patch descriptor. Rather than directly regressing an unconstrained displacement vector, a Neural Displacement Field (NDF) fuses this global patch descriptor with per-point neighborhood features. It then predicts each displacement through bounded-vector and scale factor branches, thereby achieving a superior, more uniform point distribution. Three cascaded NDF stages propagate local features and progressively refine the point positions. Experiments on PU-Net and ModelNet10 demonstrate competitive denoising accuracy and favorable inference efficiency. The source code is publicly available at this https URL.

### 10. [Constraint-Preserving Flow Matching for Spectral Graph Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05436)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Anita Yang, Ken-ichi Kawarabayashi, Yohan Jung  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Spectral graph generation models graphs through the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of their Laplacians, providing a compact and continuous alternative to direct graph construction. However, these eigensystems must retain the structural properties of graph Laplacians. Existing approaches preserve eigenvector orthonormality but do not enforce the Laplacian nullspace constraint, potentially producing eigensystems inconsistent with Laplacian structure. To address this, we introduce Laplacian-Constrained Stiefel Flow Matching (LaSFM), which preserves the nullspace condition alongside eigenvector orthonormality and positive eigenvalues during generation. We characterize the underlying constrained Stiefel manifold and formulate flow matching on it. We further extend LaSFM to property-guided generation, steering samples toward desired graph properties while maintaining these constraints. Experiments demonstrate improved performance on both spectral and graph-level metrics, along with better alignment with target properties.

### 11. [Learning Implicit Solves for Dynamic Simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05439)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Chawit Kaewnuratchadasorn, Jiaji Wang, Xin Nie  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> High-fidelity simulation of dynamical systems is central to scientific discovery and engineering design, yet remains computationally demanding. Here we introduce the Implicit Neural Operator Solver (INOS), which learns the implicit solve and encodes a time integrator. INOS pretrains a neural operator from static equilibrium solutions and reuses it within Newmark-$\beta$ integration. At each time increment, the integrator forms an effective equilibrium problem, the pretrained neural operator infers the next-step solution field, and the Newmark relations advance the dynamic state. Numerical examples in linear and nonlinear elastodynamics demonstrate accurate full-field predictions without dynamic trajectory supervision. Comparative results show that INOS, evaluated zero-shot, achieves higher predictive accuracy than neural solver baselines trained on dynamic simulations. In addition, INOS delivers over 4,600$\times$ speedup in nonlinear simulations over sequential classical finite-element solvers. INOS natively accommodates changes in dynamic conditions and enables reuse of the pretrained operator across spatial and temporal discretizations. Further evaluation demonstrates a stable million-step rollout. These findings establish INOS as a reusable neural implicit solver for accurate, efficient, and robust dynamic modeling.

### 12. [Matched but Not Better: An Equivalence-Tested Benchmark of Quantum Machine Learning on Six Clinical Datasets and the QML-Health-READY Reporting Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05442)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Mohammed Siraj B, Gousia Habib, Ruban S  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Background: Quantum machine learning (QML) is promoted for clinical prediction, but comparisons with classical models are rarely paired or tested for equivalence. Methods: We compared a quantum-kernel support vector machine (QSVM; 4 qubits, tuned bandwidth) and a variational quantum classifier (VQC) with five tuned classical models across six public clinical datasets. All models used identical four-component inputs and 25 cross-validation splits. Paired AUROC differences were assessed using a corrected resampled t-test, Holm-adjusted equivalence tests (margins: +/-0.02 and +/-0.05), and random-effects pooling. Ablation, noise, finite-shot, and implementation-fault analyses were conducted. We propose QML-Health-READY, an eight-domain reporting framework. Results: Neither quantum model significantly outperformed any classical comparator. Pooled across datasets, QSVM differed from the kernel-matched radial basis function (RBF) SVM by -0.006 AUROC (95% CI: -0.017 to +0.004), equivalent to +/-0.02. VQC differed from the best classical model by -0.007 (95% CI: -0.019 to +0.004). QSVM was equivalent to RBF SVM within +/-0.05 in four of six datasets but was significantly worse than the best classical model on NHANES 2017-18 (-0.076). A classically trivial product-state kernel performed as well as the entangling kernel. With depolarizing noise (p = 0.01) and 1,024 shots per kernel entry, QSVM AUROC decreased by 0.054 (95% CI: 0.036 to 0.071). Untuned bandwidth produced near-chance AUROC (0.48-0.59). Conclusions: Correctly configured quantum classifiers approached but did not exceed tuned classical models. Finite-shot estimation, rather than gate noise, was their main practical cost, while configuration errors could reverse conclusions. QML-Health-READY defines reporting requirements for credible claims.

### 14. [SO(3)-Equivariant Learning on CAD Boundary Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05449)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Matteo Ballegeer, Dries F. Benoit  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Learning on boundary representations (B-reps) requires robustness to the pose of a part, since CAD models are not stored in a canonical orientation. Existing B-rep networks either operate on world-frame coordinates and normals, and thus degrade under rotation, or encode each face in a local reference frame. Local frames yield invariance, but reduce inter-face messages to scalars and discard the relative orientation of adjacent faces. We propose SO3brep, an SO(3)-equivariant graph neural network on the B-rep face-adjacency graph. Node features are computed in local frames and mapped into a shared frame by Wigner D-matrices. Clebsch-Gordan tensor products couple these features with the directions between adjacent faces, so that relative orientation is preserved throughout message passing and the output remains SE(3)-invariant. Across three face-segmentation and two classification benchmarks with randomly rotated test parts, SO3brep achieves the highest accuracy among the compared B-rep networks and equivariant point cloud networks, with the fewest parameters. Its advantage is largest with few labeled parts, a setting typical of industrial CAD. Code is available at this https URL.

### 15. [Towards Robust Long-Lived Agents: Benchmarking Experience Lifecycles in Evolving Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05450)

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**

### 17. [From Activation to Intervention: Interpreting Sparse Visual Features through Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05522)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Jianyang Gu, Jacob Beattie, Mridul Khurana 等 10 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) decompose visual representations into individual features, offering a promising basis for explaining model behavior. Interpreting these features typically relies on images or pixel regions that most strongly activate them. However, this evidence becomes ambiguous when multiple features with different semantics frequently activate in the same locations, such as texture and color in the same patterns. We propose Reconstructive SAE Intervention (RecSAEI), a framework that shifts feature explanation from passive observation to active visual reconstruction. By intervening on representations along SAE decoder directions and reconstructing the visual output, we provide direct evidence of a feature's semantic meaning. We demonstrate that our method successfully disambiguates feature meanings across multiple datasets and vision encoders. Furthermore, our framework grounds [CLS] SAE interventions in a sparse set of patch SAE decoder directions. Beyond stable target response, this attribution also exposes the compositional relationship between elementary visual patterns and high-level semantics. Our results advocate for intervention reconstruction as an essential complementary signal for explaining neural networks.

### 19. [The Confidence Score: a New Measure on Kernels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05528)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Elizabeth Hofer, Martin v. Mohrenschildt  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Machine Learning

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Across a wide range of applications, kernel measures are used to tune hyperparameters, select among candidate kernels, and assess the suitability of kernels for specific learning tasks. For classification, kernel measures are used to predict which kernels may produce accurate kernel classifiers. Many existing kernel measures, such as alignment to the ideal kernel, are designed to reward kernels which captures all samples from the same class as similar. While effective in many settings, this requirement is unnecessarily restrictive for classification, where classes may exhibit substantial internal structure. In this work, the "Confidence Score", a new kernel measure, is proposed. Unlike alignment-to-the-ideal kernel measures, the Confidence Score measures the alignment between an encoding of the ground truth and that ground truth projected by the kernel, allowing kernels that preserve intra-class structure to achieve high scores. Through experiments, it is demonstrated that the Confidence Score exhibits a higher correlation with classification accuracy across a variety of kernels on both real-world and synthetic datasets, compared to select other kernel measures.

### 20. [Towards Efficient Whole Page Ranking with One-Pass Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05542)

**<font color=#1a73e8>作者：</font>** Qi Xu, Zhigang Hua, Zijie Lei 等 13 位作者  
**<font color=#188038>arXiv所属领域：</font>** Artificial Intelligence

**<font color=#5f6368>摘要：</font>**
> Whole-page ranking jointly optimizes advertisements and organic content to balance business outcomes and user engagement under complex layout and business constraints. Existing non-autoregressive (NAR) approaches enable efficient page generation but typically score candidate items independently of previously selected items, limiting their ability to capture order-dependent interactions. Autoregressive (AR) methods address this limitation through sequential conditioning, but incur substantial inference overhead that challenges large-scale deployment. Bridging this quality-efficiency gap remains a challenge in industrial whole-page ranking.
We propose Multi-Item Scoring (MIS), a prefix-aware NAR-style framework that incorporates sequential context into efficient multi-item scoring.
